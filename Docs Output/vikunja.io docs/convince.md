The pitch in 30 seconds
Vikunja is open-source task management that runs on your infrastructure. It replaces Todoist, Trello, or Asana for teams that need full control over their data.
Your team gets lists, Kanban boards, Gantt charts, and table views, with shared projects, assignments, and real-time collaboration. Enterprise features like a dedicated instance, admin tools, and a GDPR data processing agreement are available at any team size. No minimum seat count.
It's made and hosted in the EU, openly licensed under the AGPLv3, and completely bootstrapped. Funded by users, not VCs.
Why switch from what we already use?

One tool, four views

Security and compliance
Open source
Every line of Vikunja's code is publicly auditable under the AGPLv3. No hidden telemetry, no proprietary black boxes.
Made and hosted in the EU
Vikunja is built in Germany and hosted on EU infrastructure. No data transfers to the US. No CLOUD Act exposure.
GDPR-ready
Data processing agreements are available on the Enterprise plan. Self-hosted instances give you complete control over data residency.
Responsible disclosure
Security vulnerabilities are handled through a responsible disclosure process with published advisories and CVEs.
Deployment options

Run a team pilot
The best way to evaluate Vikunja is to use it with your team for two weeks. Here's how:
- Pick 5–10 people: a mix of roles that represent how your team actually works.
- Set up a test instance: self-host with Docker in 5 minutes, or start a free 14-day Cloud trial.

- Use it for real work: run your actual sprint, project, or team tasks through Vikunja for two weeks.
